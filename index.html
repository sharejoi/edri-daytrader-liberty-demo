3. Solving "The Unknown of the Spec" Across Any HCSC App

      

        

          
Upstream Discovery (edri_discover.py)

          
Instead of asking teams to guess hidden constraints from a blank page, EDRI scans legacy Java code (@Stateless EJBs, @TransactionAttribute) and tWAS configs to auto-populate nfr-passport.yaml before SDE Stage 1 starts.

        

        

          
Risk-Tiered Archetypes (1.5x vs. 2.5x)

          
Member-facing initiatives (200K+ members, Open Enrollment, legacy modernization migrations) inherit strict EJB, DB2 pool, and AI-007 human-override rules automatically—while low-risk internal tools stay lightweight.
